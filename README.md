# Panasonic Intelli-Balance 200 ↔ ESPHome

Home Assistant control of a **Panasonic Intelli-Balance 200 ERV**, in the spirit of ratgdo. A small
ESP32-C6 board sits inline on the 4-wire cable between the ERV and its FV-SW20VEC1 wall control and
runs ESPHome over Thread. The wall control keeps working as before.

What it does:

- **Shows** power, airflow, ASHRAE setting, boost, the ventilation rest cycle, and inside/outside
  temperature and humidity.
- **Controls** power, a 60 min boost, supply/exhaust airflow and the ASHRAE run time.
- **Turns the ERV back on after a power failure**, which the ERV doesn't do by itself.
- **Fails safe:** unpowered or crashed, the board passes the link straight through.

> **Wire the data lines crossed.** The terminal labelled `TXD` at the ERV and the wall control goes
> to the board's `RXD`, and `RXD` to `TXD`, on both sides of the board.
